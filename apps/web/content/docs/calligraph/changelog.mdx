## 1.4.0

Grapheme-safe text splitting via `Intl.Segmenter`, blur and scale on entering and exiting text, and a `trend` prop for directional vertical motion on the text variant.

## 1.3.0

Reworked the slots animation: clamped digit positioning, digit-based spin distance, popLayout exit with roll-out, a container-level gradient mask, and rendering without per-digit opacity.

## 1.2.0

Added the `"slots"` variant for slot-machine digit transitions and the `autoSize` prop, which animates the wrapper's width to match its content.

## 1.1.2

The number variant no longer animates currency and prefix symbols like `$` and `€` when the value changes. They slide into place without rolling, blurring or fading.

## 1.1.1

Fixed flashing during number transitions when the digit count changes, made the digit slide distance consistent in pixels, and improved how the text variant measures how much changed.

## 1.1.0

Added the `variant` prop (`"text"` or `"number"`) for rolling digit transitions, and the `animation` prop with spring presets (`"smooth"`, `"snappy"` and `"bouncy"`) inspired by SwiftUI.

## 1.0.5

Characters stagger by position, exits are faster, and drift scales with the share of characters that changed, so small edits move subtly and large rewrites get the full spread.

## 1.0.4

Default duration down to 0.38s and drift to 10px.

## 1.0.2

Better accessibility with `aria-label` and `aria-hidden`, faster diffing, and `children` narrowed to `string | number`.

## 1.0.1

Renamed from calligraphy to calligraph.

## 1.0.0

First major release.
