---
title: Limits
description: Where the caret hands back to the browser's.
---

- **Typing in another script.** While an input method is composing, like
  Japanese or Pinyin, the browser's own caret comes back and Apple's hides. It
  returns when the text is committed.
- **`email` and `number` inputs.** Browsers don't say where the caret is in
  these, so they keep their own.
- **Where it sits.** The bar lives in an empty element placed right after the
  field. It scrolls and transforms with the field, but a CSS selector like
  `:last-child` or `+` will see it.
- **Scaled fields.** A field inside a `transform: scale()` puts the caret off by
  the scale.
