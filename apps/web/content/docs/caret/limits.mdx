---
title: Limits
description: Where the caret hands back to the browser's.
---

- **Typing in another script.** While an input method is composing, like
  Japanese or Pinyin, the browser's own caret and highlight come back and
  Apple's hide. They return when the text is committed.
- **Over the text.** The highlight is drawn over the text, not behind it, so the
  text shows through it rather than on top. The browser's own highlight is
  turned off with `::selection` while the field has focus.
- **Unfocused selections.** When the field loses focus the highlight hides and
  the browser shows its own unfocused one, where it has one.
- **`email` and `number` inputs.** Browsers don't say where the caret is in
  these, so they keep their own.
- **Where it sits.** The caret and selection live in an empty element placed
  right after the field. It scrolls and transforms with the field, but a CSS selector like
  `:last-child` or `+` will see it.
- **Scaled fields.** A field inside a `transform: scale()` puts the caret and
  selection off by the scale.
